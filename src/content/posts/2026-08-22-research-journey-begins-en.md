This site documents what I study during my graduate programme, how research questions develop, and how experiments, challenges, and iterations eventually lead to research outcomes.

The first stage focuses on building the necessary foundations and refining potential research topics.

I am currently considering three directions for my Master's thesis:

- Context-Aware Sensitive Information Detection
- Early Risk Detection through Multi-Log Correlation
- Offline Generative AI Learning Support System

As the research topic becomes more focused and experimental work begins, I plan to document the process chronologically wherever possible.
