## Factual reconciliation

- At 12:20 the spoken advice broadly requests disclosure for even syntax and
  grammar assistance. At 21:54 the speaker and actual slide exempt basic
  spelling/grammar/editing. At 23:17 he personally recommends broader disclosure.
  The Note explicitly distinguishes that recommendation from official policy.
  Wiley's accessible ethics guidance was checked on 27 October: substantial
  editing/development/translation requires disclosure, solely spelling/grammar/
  general editing is exempt, human authors remain accountable. Individual
  journal placement/format remains authoritative; methods-only is not imposed
  for every use of writing assistance.
- The speaker calls independently written text falsely flagged by a detector
  a false negative. The phenomenon is a false positive; the Note uses 오탐 /
  false positives without reproducing the mistaken label. Turnitin's primary
  guide confirms possible misidentification and distinguishes AI percentages
  from independent text-similarity scores. No numerical safe/unsafe threshold
  is inferred from the speaker's examples, and AI score is not proof of validity.
- Do not adopt blanket spoken assertions that retrieval-based AI cannot
  hallucinate, that all named models embed immediately detectable watermarks,
  or that editors universally screen/reject papers on an AI-detection score.
  None was established by the sources checked. No current capability claims
  for named AI products were needed to explain the writing workflow.
- The claimed mathematical/physics breakthroughs around 44:16 were not
  independently investigated and are omitted as unrelated to practical writing.
- Correct obvious transcript errors against context/slides: Brief, non-standard
  abbreviations, retrieval-augmented generation (not the ASR expansion), ChatGPT,
  Wiley, Authorea, ORCID. The public Note does not need every brand mentioned.
- Abstract sentence counts and keyword allocation are presentation advice, not
  universal journal rules. The 20-second idea is a planning heuristic, not a
  timed exercise performed by the author. No unpublished manuscript, actual
  AI usage, test, outcome or model version invented.
- Language editing resources are described by purpose, without turning an ASR
  “four free professional services” phrase into a promise of free editing.
